# Frame — Meta marketing plan

**Written 2026-11-23. Binding on every post written into `inbox/`.**
Read this *with* `CONTENT-RULES.md`, not instead of it. The rules say what may be
said. This plan says what the posting is *for*.

---

## 1. What this plan is for

Frame's turnover is **£13,000 per week** (owner's stated figure, 2026-11-05 —
his statement, not a measured till read). The target is **£25,000**. The gap is
**£12,000 a week**.

That gap does not close with followers. It closes with three things, and this
plan exists to serve exactly those three:

| # | Target | Why Meta is the right channel for it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Private hire enquiries** | Highest value per transaction by a wide margin. Nobody searches Google for a Christmas party *before they have the idea* — Meta is where the idea is planted, in a catchment Frame can actually serve |
| 2 | **The weekday 8–10am hole** | Nine of the ten emptiest open hours in Frame's week are 8am or 9am. The room already exists and is already staffed |
| 3 | **Average transaction value** | Reaching the people who already come, with what they are not ordering |

**Anything that serves none of these three is decoration.** A post that gets
likes and moves none of them has cost a slot that could have moved one.

| Channel | State | What it gets |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** `@framefinsbury` | 2,840 followers, 344 posts — *as read on 2026-09-25*. Not re-verifiable today: Instagram now serves a login wall to an anonymous fetch, so treat this as a dated snapshot, not a current number | **The workhorse.** Everything original is made for Instagram first |

The gap between 2,840 and 9 is the whole argument. Effort follows the audience
that exists.

## 3. What Meta can move at Frame — and what it cannot

**Saturday noon is the busiest hour of Frame's week, indexed at 100.** Sunday
1pm is 85. **Never push traffic into a full room.** Doing so costs money twice:
it spends reach on people who would have come anyway, and it worsens the
experience of people already inside.

So the posting pushes *away* from the peak:

| Daypart | Busyness index | Posting job |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon–Fri 8–10am** | 16–37 (Monday 8am = **16**, emptiest hour of the week) | **Target 2.** The morning as a named, repeatable thing |
| Mon–Fri 2–5pm | 31–48 | Secondary. Work-from-café, quiet-room angle |
| **Sat 11am–3pm** | 81–100 | **Do not advertise into this.** Use it only to *show* the room full for private-hire credibility |
| Sun 11am–3pm | 68–85 | Near-peak. Same rule |

*(Busyness is Google's public relative index, 0–100, measured 2026-11-12. It is
not covers and not turnover. It says when the room is full relative to itself.)*

**What Meta genuinely cannot do here, and no plan should pretend otherwise:**
it cannot make a cold stranger appear at 8am on a Monday. What it can do is
make the morning *known* to the 2,840 who already follow — who are overwhelmingly
local and have mostly already been in — and to a tight local radius. That is a
habit-forming job, measured in weeks, not a response-rate job measured in days.

## 4. The posting system

**Three posts a week, plus stories.** Not daily. A café posting daily runs out
of truthful things to say by week two and starts posting filler, which trains
the audience to scroll past.

**Weekly shape:**

| Slot | Day | Serves | Form |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Mon or Tue | **Target 2 — the morning** | Single image or short video, the room quiet and the coffee being made |
| B | Thu | **Target 1 — private hire** | The room, a group in it, or the occasion pages' subject matter |
| C | Sat or Sun | **Target 3 — ATV / menu depth** | One thing people are not ordering. Never a price |

**Stories:** as often as there is something true to show. Stories carry the
day-to-day; the grid carries the three targets.

**Every post carries exactly one action**, from the four permitted destinations
in `README.md` — ORDER, BOOK, MENU, ENQUIRE. A post with no action is a
decoration. A post with two actions gets neither.

**Where posts come from:** written into `inbox/` in this repository, one file
per post. A file in `inbox/` is a **proposal**. The owner approves before
anything is published. Nothing here goes live automatically.

## 5. The twelve weeks

Seasonal reality on the day this was written: **it is 23 November.** Christmas
party booking season is already most of the way through. The December occasions
are being decided now, not in December.

| Weeks | Dominant theme | Why now |
|---|---|---|
| **Now – 20 Dec** | **Private hire, heavily weighted** — Christmas parties, work parties and away-days, birthdays | The only weeks of the year when the demand is already in people's heads. Slot B every week, plus one extra slot borrowed from C |
| 21 Dec – 1 Jan | Hours and presence only | Post what is true about opening over the period, nothing else. Never state a festive hour that has not been confirmed |
| 2 Jan – mid Feb | **The morning**, weighted hard | January is when a new morning habit is actually formed. Slot A gets the extra weight that December took |
| Mid Feb – end | Balanced A/B/C, with birthdays and christenings carrying private hire | Occasions become year-round once Christmas is gone |

The six occasion pages — Christmas, birthday, work parties and away-days,
christenings and baby showers, wakes and funeral receptions, matchday — are the
landing destinations for slot B. They are real pages with real content. Use them.

## 6. Paid Meta — gated, and honestly so

**No money is spent under this plan today.** Two separate reasons, both real:

**First, it cannot be measured.** Four Meta identifiers are recorded as UNKNOWN
in this project's configuration — the Business portfolio, the Page, the
Instagram account and the ad account. Until the owner names them, no read of
Meta's own numbers can be proven to be Frame's rather than another business's.
A figure that cannot be proved to be Frame's is not a figure.

**Second, there is a worked lesson in this project's own records.** Google Ads
spent **£77.09** for **223 clicks at £0.346** and **zero attributed
conversions**. The cause was not the budget. A click at that price in London is
display inventory, and with no conversion signal the campaign optimised toward
the cheapest clicks it could find — a closed loop that more budget accelerates
rather than breaks. **Meta will do the same thing for the same reason.**

**What has to be true before a single pound goes in:**

1. The owner names the Business portfolio, Page, Instagram account and ad account, and each is confirmed to be Frame's.
2. A countable objective exists that Meta can actually see — a private-hire enquiry landing on a page we control, not a walk-in and not a Deliveroo order.
3. A budget cap and a stop rule are agreed **in writing, in advance**.
4. The owner approves the spend. Spending is his decision, not this plan's.

Until all four hold, paid Meta is a proposal, not a channel.

## 7. Measurement — what Meta can and cannot see

| Customer action | Does Meta see it? |
|---|---|
| Sees a post, walks in | **No** |
| Deliveroo order | **No** — it leaves for a third party |
| Dojo / Reserve-with-Google booking | **No** — not connected as a conversion |
| Visits the website from a post | Yes, if the link carries a tag |
| Sends a DM or an enquiry | Yes |
| Saves or shares a post | Yes |

So the honest success measure per target:

- **Target 1 (private hire):** enquiries received — DMs plus enquiries from the private-hire page. A countable number.
- **Target 2 (the morning):** the 8–10am busyness index, re-read from Google's public data every few weeks. Slow, public, free, and not gameable by us.
- **Target 3 (ATV):** not measurable without the till. **UNKNOWN until the owner supplies till data.** Say so rather than inventing a proxy.

**Saves and shares are the only engagement numbers worth reading.** Likes are not
a business signal and are not reported upward.

## 8. The constraint that gates all of it

**Images.** This project holds no photographs with verified usage rights. Captions
can be written here all day; a post still needs a picture, and publishing one
whose rights are unverified is the one reliable way to turn a free channel into
an expensive one.

So: **captions come from this repository, images come from the owner or from an
in-house shoot.** The shot list already exists in the project
(`09-creative/PHOTO-SHOT-LIST.md`, `09-creative/PHOTO-VIDEO-BRIEF.md`). A post
written here with no image available stays `status: draft` and waits. It is not
published with a stock photograph.

A second, smaller gate: three dish photographs currently in use show food that
is no longer on the menu. They must not be reused.

---

## 9. What this plan deliberately does not do

- **It does not promise a follower number.** Follower growth is not one of the three targets and is not reported as a result.
- **It does not build for Facebook.** Nine followers. Mirror and move on.
- **It does not touch TikTok.** No verified account state, no evidence, and the three targets do not need it. Revisit when one of them is being served and the other two are not.
- **It does not publish anything by itself.** Approval is the owner's, every time.
